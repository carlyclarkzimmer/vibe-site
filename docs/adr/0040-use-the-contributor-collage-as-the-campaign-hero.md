# ADR 0040: Use the contributor collage as the 2026 campaign hero

## Status

Accepted

## Context

The `/beyond-the-bottleneck-2026` landing page needed to foreground the full
group of audio-series contributors while keeping the campaign's opening copy
and CTA clear of the portraits.

## Decision

- Use the approved group portrait as the full-bleed campaign hero image.
- Keep the campaign hero component boundary and existing registration anchor.
- Arrange the free-series label, title, subheader, and CTA as a centered
  editorial composition inside the portrait's built-in negative space.
- Keep the hero in the normal page flow so it scrolls away with the rest of the
  landing page.

## Consequences

The campaign now leads with the contributors as a collective while preserving
the existing brand typography, gold hairlines, CTA behavior, page structure,
and form integration. Future hero-image replacements should retain generous
negative space above the subjects or revisit the overlay spacing at desktop and
mobile breakpoints.
