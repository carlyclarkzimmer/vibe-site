# ADR 0024: Streamline the homepage close

## Status

Accepted

## Context

The homepage previously placed a full “Calm. Exact. Practical.” process section
between the services showcase and the closing testimonial. Carly requested that
the complete process section be removed and that the testimonial quote use a
quieter scale.

## Decision

Remove the process section and its route-owned content from the homepage. Keep
the existing closing testimonial, but reduce its display quote size by 50%.
The compact uppercase attribution remains unchanged so it stays legible.

## Consequences

The homepage now moves directly from the services showcase into social proof.
The testimonial reads as a quieter closing endorsement rather than another
large display-type section.
