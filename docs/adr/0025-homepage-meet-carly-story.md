# ADR 0025: Add the homepage “Meet Carly” story

## Status

Accepted

## Context

The homepage moved directly from the bottleneck framework into the services
showcase. Carly requested an editorial founder-story section between those two
moments so visitors understand her lived connection to the work before seeing
the available offers.

## Decision

Add a route-owned “Meet Carly” section immediately before “Work With Carly.”
Use Carly’s approved seated teal-jacket portrait, a large uppercase serif
heading, and Carly’s supplied story copy in a spacious two-column composition.
Present the photograph at its full natural aspect ratio rather than cropping it.
Split the final two paragraphs into a full-width closing text block beneath the
main story-and-image row. On mobile, stack the portrait beneath the story copy
and retain left alignment.

## Consequences

The homepage now establishes personal credibility before presenting services.
The section reuses existing photography and shared brand tokens without adding
new dependencies or changing other site routes.
