# ADR 0036: Reframe the About page around Business Restoration

## Status

Accepted

## Decision

Keep `/about` as a permanent main-site page and retain its immersive
photographic hero, shared navigation, and established Bodoni, EB Garamond, and
Montserrat type system. Recompose the page around the Business Restoration
narrative: the visitor problem, Carly's role and promises, the three-phase
method, Carly's personal origin story, personal biography, and a final path to
the Services page.

Use clearly labeled editorial image placeholders for photography Carly has not
yet supplied. Keep those placeholders semantic and visually intentional so
they can be replaced with approved images without changing the page structure.

## Consequences

- The About page explains Carly's method as well as her personal story.
- All supplied copy remains route-owned and appears without invented claims.
- Future photography can replace placeholders in place.
- The route continues to use the shared main-site header and footer.
