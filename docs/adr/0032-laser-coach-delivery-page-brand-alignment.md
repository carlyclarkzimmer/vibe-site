# ADR 0032: Align the Laser Coach delivery page with its landing page

## Status

Accepted

## Decision

Keep the existing 5-Minute Laser Coach delivery route, installation link,
walkthrough video, support details, and approved body copy while moving the
route from the legacy shared stylesheet to a route-owned CSS Module.

Use the Breakthrough landing page's established visual language: soft-gray
welcome and founder sections, a black benefits section, editorial display
type, generous section spacing, and lime calls to action that become pink with
white text on hover. Preserve responsive wrapping and visible keyboard focus.

## Consequences

- The landing and delivery experiences now feel like one campaign journey.
- Delivery-page styling can evolve without affecting unrelated legacy routes.
- The video, ChatGPT installation destination, contact links, and campaign
  functionality remain unchanged.
