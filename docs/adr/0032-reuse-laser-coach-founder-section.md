# ADR 0032: Reuse the Laser Coach founder treatment on delivery

## Status

Accepted

## Decision

Replace the legacy Carly biography on the 5-Minute Laser Coach delivery route
with the approved founder copy and two-column portrait treatment established on
the Breakthrough landing page.

Keep the delivery page's existing hero, walkthrough, benefits, and installation
flow unchanged. Point the founder-section call to action directly to the same
ChatGPT installation destination used elsewhere on the delivery page.

## Consequences

- Visitors see consistent founder positioning before and after opting in.
- The delivery page retains its existing structure outside the founder section.
- The section collapses to one column on mobile while preserving the approved
  photography crop through CSS.
