# ADR 0011: Editorial About origin story

## Status

Accepted

## Decision

The About page is a route-owned editorial narrative built around Carly's New
Year's Eve origin story. It uses the approved airport photograph in the hero,
then alternates large editorial statements, long-form copy, and explicit photo
placeholders.

The page inherits the main-site navigation, site-wide equity pledge, and legal
footer. Its layout follows the established homepage language: high-contrast
serif typography, selective berry-pink emphasis, generous whitespace, fine
rules, and responsive image overlays.

## Consequences

- Distinctive About-page prose remains close to its owning route.
- Photo placeholders remain visible until Carly supplies approved images.
- Replacing placeholders requires image files, approved placement, and useful
  alt text.
- The page does not introduce a generic content builder or new dependency.
