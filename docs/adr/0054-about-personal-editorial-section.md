# ADR 0054: Add a personal editorial section to the About page

## Status

Accepted

## Context

The About page explains Carly’s business philosophy and professional approach,
but needs a distinct personal chapter that shows the life the work is intended
to protect. The final photography is still being selected, so the composition
must be reviewable without presenting stock imagery as approved brand content.

## Decision

Add a route-owned personal editorial section between the About bio and closing
call to action. The section uses alternating two-column stories, a three-image
gallery, and a closing statement. Clearly labeled, accessible placeholders hold
the approved image proportions until Carly supplies the final photographs.

Use warm ivory for the personal section, soft blush for the About page’s larger
feature surfaces, charcoal for primary copy, and berry for restrained labels,
rules, and calls to action. The section continues to use Bodoni Moda, EB
Garamond, and Montserrat according to the established site typography roles.

## Consequences

- Final images can be swapped in without changing the section structure.
- The About page gains a clear bridge from professional biography to its final
  call to action.
- Desktop uses editorial two- and three-column compositions; tablet and mobile
  collapse into a natural reading order without a carousel.
- The page remains route-owned rather than introducing a generic content
  schema or shared component before reuse is demonstrated.
