# ADR 0031: Align Contact typography with the main site

## Status

Accepted

## Decision

Keep the Contact page's approved content and email behavior,
while aligning its typography with the main site. Use Bodoni Moda at the shared
editorial display scale for the page heading, EB Garamond at the shared body
scale for contact and solidarity copy, and Montserrat for interface-style link
emphasis.

Present the Contact hero as one seamless full-width black section with the
contact information centered in white. Omit photography and do not use a
separate floating contact box.

Integrate the shared navigation with the Contact hero and give its primary
"Book a call" action the same white outline and inverted hover treatment used
over the Home, Services, and About heroes.

Increase the contact information body copy by 20%. Remove the route-owned
solidarity section from Contact; this page continues to suppress the shared
equity pledge so the removed statement is not duplicated below the hero.

## Consequences

- Contact now shares the same headline and body-copy rhythm as Home, Services,
  and About.
- Existing email links remain unchanged.
- The centered black treatment stays consistent across desktop and mobile.
- The Contact navigation and primary action now use the established hero
  treatment.
- The Contact page ends after its primary contact section.
- Mobile typography continues to scale without reverting to a separate font
  system.
